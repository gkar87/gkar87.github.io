Published work

Nine lines of work from my papers and theses, each with its key result.

Connected vehicles: sensing, identity and trust

Catching cars that jam GPS, identifying the driver from how they start the car, and estimating traffic from dash cams.

100% of GPS-jamming cars identified with two roadside points Read more →

Extending what a car can see and sense

Sharing sensor views between cars to see past the truck ahead, and sensing the road from phones and cars.

>90% lane-change accuracy from phone and car sensors Read more →

Scoring and coaching drivers

Real-time driver scoring from in-vehicle data, and gamified feedback that makes the score matter.

>95% agreement between real and simulated trips Read more →

Hybrid quantum-classical intrusion detection

One wavelet, CNN and quantum-circuit framework that detects DDoS traffic and classifies Android malware.

99.86% DDoS detection accuracy Read more →

Quantum machine learning for Raman spectroscopy

Quanvolutional and hybrid networks that classify Raman spectra of serum and of weathered microplastics.

Run on an IonQ quantum processor Read more →

IoT device identity and network incidents

Telling which device sent a packet, and which attack is under way, from packet headers alone.

95.3% of devices identified from a single packet Read more →

Acoustic side channels on phones

A phone behind your keyboard can hear which keys you press, with no training data.

94% of keystrokes recovered Read more →

Explainable and applied AI

Explainable models for food fraud and employee attrition, crime prediction, and Turkish text classification.

96% accuracy classifying Turkish scientific texts Read more →

Sharing spectrum fairly (M.S. thesis)

Splitting a cell's subcarriers between its center and its edge, so the network is both fast and fair.

≈5% better throughput × fairness with adaptive splits Read more →
