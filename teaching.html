Courses and course sites

Teaching

Students learn computing by building it on real problems.

Every graduate course I teach comes with interactive, browser-based notes, so students can run SQL, Python, and algorithms live as they read, with nothing to install. In machine learning that means training and debugging on a messy, real dataset; in algorithms, proving a time bound and then watching it in running code; in databases, designing a schema that survives real queries. My research comes into the classroom too: intrusion detection, IoT fingerprinting and connected vehicles are case studies, and Advanced Algorithms opens by turning GPS navigation into a graph and rerunning Dijkstra's algorithm as traffic changes.
