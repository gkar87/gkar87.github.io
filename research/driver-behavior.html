Scotto: scoring driving in real time

Insurers and fleet operators score drivers, but often from coarse data, after the trip. Scotto (IEEE VTC2019-Spring), with B. Asiroglu and F. S. Bir, scores driver behavior in real time from the car's own in-vehicle data, so feedback can arrive while it still matters. The live figure above shows the basic idea with made-up thresholds: flag harsh braking and harsh starts from acceleration, and turn them into a score.

Gamification: making the score matter

A score alone rarely changes how someone drives. In Improving driver behavior using gamification (MobiWIS 2018), with S. Helvaci, A. Senova and S. Gören, we read in-vehicle data, scored driving errors on several criteria, and wrapped the score in achievements and leaderboards, with a feature that points out nearby places of interest. We tested it on two real-world trips and compared them with simulated ones.
