What a quanvolution layer does

A classical convolution slides a small filter over its input. A quanvolution layer slides a small quantum circuit instead: each window of input values is encoded as rotations of a few qubits, the circuit entangles them, and the measured expectation values become a new feature map that a classical network then reads. The live figure above computes one such feature exactly for a toy circuit.

  1. Windowa few neighboring spectrum values
  2. Encodeeach value rotates one qubit
  3. Entanglea small fixed or trained circuit
  4. Measureexpectation values become features
  5. Classifya classical network reads the feature map

COVID-19 from the Raman spectra of serum

In IEEE Access (2025), with S. S. Dutta, S. Sandeep, S. Sridevi, R. Mistry and J. Ortega, we turned Raman spectra of human serum into wavelet scaleograms and classified COVID-19 with a quanvolutional neural network, comparing it with classical CNNs. It is the same wavelet-to-quantum pipeline we use for intrusion detection, applied to light instead of traffic.

Weathered microplastics on a real quantum processor

In AVS Quantum Science (2026), with H. G. Kulkarni, A. R. Banerjee, T. Mandloi, S. Sridevi and S. Geetha, a classical-quantum neural network with dual-channel feature extraction ran on an IonQ quantum processor to classify weathered microplastics from their Raman spectra. Running on hardware, not only a simulator, is what makes the results a test of today's machines.

Discussion

What it shows

Raman spectra are a natural test bed for small quantum models, and the microplastics study ran its hybrid network on real quantum hardware, not only in simulation.

Where it falls short

  • Spectral datasets are small, so results depend on careful validation.
  • Time on quantum hardware is limited and costly, so the number of runs is small.
  • The classical parts of the pipeline do much of the work; isolating what the quantum part adds needs matched baselines.

What it means

Real-hardware runs are where the field needs evidence, and they are now part of how my group works.

Open research questions

Questions this work leaves open. They are directions, not results; each links to the planned project that takes it up.
